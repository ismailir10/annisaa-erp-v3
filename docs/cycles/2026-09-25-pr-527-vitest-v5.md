## Context

PR #527 upgrades the development test runner `vitest` from 4.1.10 to 5.0.1. Its original staging base was `5037dac26002d1235dd3c93b361bcba326d1df9b`. PR #530 independently proposed coverage-v8 5.0.1, but its exact peer dependency makes that bump inseparable from #527. Keep #530 open until #527 merges and the final lockfile confirms all of #530's unique dependency changes are present.
